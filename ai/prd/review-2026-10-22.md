
## 5. Attach is the primary live mode (changes sections 10.1 and 12)

- Add a source row to §10.1: **Attach**: the observatory watches `~/.factory/sessions/` with a filesystem notifier, detects any new session JSONL (a mission started from any terminal on this machine), tails it as it grows and renders it in real time, named by its `--tag` or session id. Same adapter as replay (a file; replay is attach at end-of-file).
- §12: attach moves into **M1** with replay; **M2** becomes hosting only (`droid exec --mission -o stream-jsonrpc` spawned from the app, with pause, message and answer controls). M1 acceptance: start a mission in a terminal, see its events on the board within 1 s.
- Reason: missions are started from the terminal in practice; a launch screen is the secondary path. Dogfooding starts the moment M1 lands.
