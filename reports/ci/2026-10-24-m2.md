## Critical path and the BACKLOG row

`rust` and `coverage` run in parallel and `rust` is the longer in every M2 run (152 against 142 s, 122 against 100 s,
189 against 167 s, 139 against 124 s), so the wall time is `rust` plus the 10 to 15 s of queue and aggregate. Splitting
the coverage job apart from the Rust job (its own workflow, not in `mission-checks`) would save, per run,
`max(0, coverage - rust)`: **0 s in all four M2 runs** (the only run where it would have saved anything is the M1 pass
run `40062540207`, 149 s coverage against 113 s rust, 36 s). The duplicated core tests are the lever: `coverage` re-runs
what `rust`'s `cargo test -p observatory-core -p observatory-cli` (71 to 92 s) already ran. Taking the core tests out
of `rust` moves the critical path to `coverage`, and the saving is bounded by `rust - coverage` of each run: 10 s (merge),
22 s (`36a042e`), 22 s (`8ab88e2`), 15 s (`bf2f137`), mean 17 s. Both bounds are far from the 3 min budget lever
(`coverage` itself, 100 to 142 s, `llvm-cov` step 110 s). BACKLOG B-056 records this with these computed bounds.
The workflow is not changed in this pass.
