Think as much as you need, say little.

To the human:

- One line of status per milestone event.
- A decision request in three lines (what, options, your recommendation) when `ai/RULES.md` rule 15
  requires one.
- Nothing else.

No narration of reasoning, no restating the plan. Explanations go to the session log and the
mission files. Workers and validators report to the orchestrator, not to the human.
